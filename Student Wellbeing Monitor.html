Course groups were standardised from free text (e.g. Engine, KOE → Engineering; BCS, BIT → Computing/IT; Fiqh, Usuluddin → Islamic Studies). Abbreviations such as KOE and KENMS are my assumption. Mathemathics, CTS and MHSC sit in “Other / unclear”.
